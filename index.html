Brand-buy rows excluded from CPC

Some campaigns are sold on impressions, not clicks. They bill a flat fee, collect almost no clicks, and produce an implied cost-per-click in the tens of dollars. Averaging those into a CPC figure lets a single sponsorship invoice swing an entire month. Any row whose revenue divided by clicks exceeds A$2.00 is treated as one of these and held out of every CPC calculation.
